## Teaching and Progressive Project Levels

Some repositories were intentionally organised as **Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3** projects.

This structure reflects my experience in designing and delivering progressive technical projects for vocational learners. The projects were structured to support learners at different stages of technical readiness:

- **Level 1 – Foundation:** data preparation, SQL, validation and descriptive analysis;
- **Level 2 – Applied:** dashboards, statistical analysis, multi-variable modelling and analytical workflows;
- **Level 3 – Advanced:** APIs, databases, monitoring, uncertainty analysis, automated testing and deployable services.

The project levels therefore represent increasing technical depth and defined learning outcomes. They do not indicate incomplete or duplicated projects.

Some newer repositories combine the foundational, applied and advanced stages into a single integrated professional platform.
